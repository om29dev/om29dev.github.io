About

Engineering with Purpose

Focused on architecting reliable systems, clean developer tooling, and applied machine learning solutions that solve concrete problems.

I am an Information Technology undergraduate at Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCOE), Pune. My technical work spans full-stack backend development, cross-platform mobile engineering with Flutter, and edge-deployable AI models.

My philosophy centers on simplicity, system performance, and shipping functional software. Rather than building speculative demos, I develop solutions addressing specific use cases: accessibility in civic services through voice assistance (Civic Voice), mobile Android development environments (Nivora), precision agriculture weed detection (WeedSight), and industrial hazard telemetry (NIRIKSHA).

I actively explore distributed system principles, database optimization, and local-first AI pipelines capable of operating efficiently with minimal latency.
